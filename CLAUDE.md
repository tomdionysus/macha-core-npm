## CodeGraph

This repository is indexed by CodeGraph (`.codegraph/`). Use `codegraph_explore` (MCP) or `codegraph explore "<query>"` (shell) for every question about code before grep, find or reading files: locating symbols, callers, call paths, and the source you are about to edit. Grep and Read are for non-code text (CSS, docs, env files, changelog) and for exact-text edits after CodeGraph has shown the source.
